# UX audit, 16 October 2026 (legacy web app, before Phase 3)

How it was done: the backend ran locally with the real Sentinel-2 cache applied (`scripts/fetch_satellite.py --from-cache`) and live
Open-Meteo weather, plus a second server on a fresh seeded database (simulated vigor, four tasks) to see a busy land view.
Every route was captured at 390 px and 1440 px in English, French and Arabic with a headless Chromium, with the Copilot in its
thinking and answered states, the floating Copilot on a phone, and the home page offline. Screenshots and the measurements
(overflow, touch targets, unlabeled buttons, console errors) are in `2026-10-16-before/`.

Measured: no horizontal overflow on any page, no console error, no unlabeled button. Touch targets under 44 px: language buttons
(36 px tall), day strip (43 px wide), zone chips A to D (42 px wide), `<summary>` rows (32 px tall), brand link (26 px), map pins
(about 15 px on a phone).

Severity: **3** must fix (a rule of the project or WCAG A is broken), **2** should fix (clear friction for the farmer), **1** polish.
"Rule" names the project rule from the brief (48 px targets, one primary action, honest labels, three languages, offline first,
numbers only from the server) or the WCAG criterion or Nielsen heuristic.

## Copilot and voice

| # | Screen | Problem | Rule broken | Sev | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | Copilot (all pages) | States today: idle (violet mic), listening (red pulse), thinking (three dots), **speaking (nothing)**, error (hint for 3.5 s), offline (mic at 40 % opacity with a tooltip no phone can show). ## Not measured here
- A real phone (Android, Chrome, HTTPS), a real microphone and a noisy place: not available in this session, so V2 is a design, not a tested result.
- Screen reader runs (only roles and names were checked in the DOM).
- 200 % zoom: 1440 px at 200 % falls into the phone layout, which has no overflow at 390 px; text-only zoom was not tested.
